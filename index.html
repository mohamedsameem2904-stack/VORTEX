About VORTEX

Founded in Bengaluru, India, VORTEX was born out of a relentless passion for high-performance gaming hardware. We engineer and curate elite gaming PCs, custom keyboards, high-DPI mice, and spatial audio headsets designed to give competitive gamers and creators the absolute edge.

Our mission is simple: to exhale smoothness into every frame, every keystroke, and every victory. Backed by rigorous benchmark testing, 3-year onsite warranties, and lightning-fast nationwide delivery, VORTEX is your definitive destination for pro-grade battle stations.
